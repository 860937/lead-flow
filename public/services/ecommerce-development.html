The Secret to High-Yield Indian E-Commerce

In the Indian market, e-commerce stores fail primarily due to two reasons: sluggish mobile loading speeds that cause over 60% of shoppers to abandon before the product page renders, and complicated multi-step checkouts that lack native UPI payment integration.

Lead Flow engineers lightning-fast shopping experiences. We optimize critical rendering paths, streamline the checkout to a single seamless step, and integrate instant payment modes such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, and net banking via Razorpay and Cashfree.
