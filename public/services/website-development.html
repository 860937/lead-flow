Our Development Stack & Technologies

We employ a robust, battle-tested modern tech stack designed for security, longevity, and simplicity:

  • Frontend: Semantic HTML5, modern CSS3 (Flexbox, CSS Grid, Custom Properties), vanilla ES6+ JavaScript.
  • Backend Engine: Node.js + Express with strict Content Security Policy (CSP), rate-limiting, and sanitized inputs.
  • Database & Storage: SQLite with WAL mode for ultra-fast localized lead storage, easily exportable to CSV or migratable to Postgres.
  • Typography & Assets: Poppins, Inter, and Google Noto Sans Tamil; WebP compressed responsive graphics.
