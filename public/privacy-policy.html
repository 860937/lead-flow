Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 | Last Updated: November 22, 2026

Lead Flow ("we", "our", or "us"), operating in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India, is dedicated to protecting the privacy and personal data of our website visitors, clients, and prospective business partners. This Privacy Policy outlines our data collection, processing, and storage practices in strict compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 (and associated SPDI Rules).

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to evaluate your project scope and provide accurate service proposals:

  • Contact Information: Full name, verified mobile/WhatsApp telephone number, and business email address.
  • Business Details: Company name, selected service of interest, estimated project budget, and project requirements.
  • Technical & Analytics Data: IP address, browser type, referral URLs, and UTM campaign parameters, collected strictly after cookie consent.

2. Lawful Basis & Purpose of Processing

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, personal data is processed exclusively on the basis of your affirmative, informed consent for the following specified business purposes:

  • To contact you via phone call, WhatsApp message, or email regarding your requested quotation or consultation.
  • To prepare project proposals, deliverable milestones, and technical architecture plans.
  • To prevent malicious bot activity, spam submissions, and fraudulent access via honeypots and rate limiters.

3. No Sale or Third-Party Sharing of Personal Data

We never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your contact information to third-party telemarketers or ad brokers. Your data is accessed solely by authorized Lead Flow technical engineers for project delivery.

4. Security & Data Retention

All lead data is stored in secured, parameterized SQLite databases located in high-grade data centers with strict encryption in transit (HTTPS / TLS 1.3) and restricted administrative access behind bcrypt authentication. Lead records are retained for a maximum of 24 months for commercial warranty, or until a request for erasure is submitted.

5. Your Rights as a Data Principal (DPDP Act, 2023)

As an Indian citizen and data principal, you hold the following statutory rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a full copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: Request immediate rectification of inaccurate or outdated contact details.
  • Right to Erasure (Deletion): Request permanent deletion of your inquiry and phone records from our database.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke consent at any time without affecting past lawful processing.
