Client Terms

Terms of Service & Engagement

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Project Initiation & Milestones

All web development projects require an initial 50% mobilization advance payment before architectural design and coding commences. The remaining 50% milestone payment becomes due upon staging completion and client sign-off, prior to final deployment on the client's production domain.

2. Intellectual Property & Code Ownership

Lead Flow grants full, irrevocable 100% intellectual property and source code ownership to the client upon receipt of full final project payment. The client is free to host, modify, or migrate their codebase to any server provider without restriction.

3. Scope, Revisions & Delivery Timelines

Each package includes defined revision rounds (Starter: 1 round; Business: 2 rounds; Growth: 3 rounds). Revisions encompass adjustments to layouts, text, color schemes, and image assets within the initial agreed scope. Substantial structural changes or additions outside the original scope are quoted separately at standard rates.

4. Third-Party Hosting & Domain Services

Domain registration and server hosting fees are direct third-party vendor charges payable by the client. While Lead Flow configures DNS and provides setup assistance, ongoing domain renewal and hosting renewal obligations remain with the client.

5. Limitation of Liability & Jurisdiction

Lead Flow is not liable for indirect damages, lost profits, or data loss caused by third-party hosting downtime or external cyber incidents. All commercial disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India.
